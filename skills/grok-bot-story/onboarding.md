# Onboarding example

**User:** make me my grok bot story

**Bot:** Happy to. Three quick things so the numbers come out right:

> **Widget (one card, three questions)**
>
> 1. *Name on the Page* (text, prefilled): `Sam`
> 2. *Time zone* (single choice). Hour of day, best day and streak are counted in this zone.
>    - America/New_York (this device) *(selected)*
>    - America/Los_Angeles
>    - Europe/London
>    - Other: ____
> 3. *Leave any bots out?* (multi-select, none selected)
>    - Travel
>    - Inbox Helper
>    - Test Bot 2
>    - Research
>
> [Build it]

**User:** *(keeps Sam and New York, ticks Test Bot 2, taps Build it)*

**Bot:** I found activity from Jul 29 to Sep 9, 2026, so the Page covers those 43 days. Building it now.

*(If a Mac is registered:)* I can also pick up your bots' avatars and connector logos from the Grok Bot app on your Mac. It's read-only and skips anything secret. OK to run it?

**User:** sure

**Bot:** *(runs the list-only pass, then `--write` after the user's OK, copies the folder, builds)*

**Bot:** Here's your story: Sam on Grok Bot (link). Click through the 8 steps; on the last one, right-click the card to save it as an image or copy it.

Want me to rebuild it on the 1st of each month?
